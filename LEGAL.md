# Legal / repository policy

This repository contains compatibility recipes only.

It does not contain:

- game binaries
- CD/DVD/floppy images
- extracted game assets
- installed game directories
- Wine prefixes
- cracks
- serials
- no-CD patches
- copyrighted assets

Users must provide their own legally obtained copies of the games.

Links in recipe files are references for identification, purchase, preservation, or archival research. Availability and legal status may vary by country.
